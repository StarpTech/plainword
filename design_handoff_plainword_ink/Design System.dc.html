Plainword Ink

A warm editorial system for a writing tool. Paper surfaces, a forest-ink accent, serif for anything that is writing, mono for anything that is machinery. Corrections read like an editor's marks: red pencil out, green ink in.

05 · Rules of motion

180 ms
One duration for everything the user watches: content swaps, panel resizes, receipt expansion. Ease-out, 4 px rise.
arrive · settle
Streamed text is never torn down when it finishes — the same words gain their controls in place. Applying sweeps green ink across the field, then everything gets out of the way.
reduce motion
Every animation here is decoration. With Reduce Motion on, text appears whole, the caret holds steady, and panels cut rather than glide.
